# Compliance and Control Evidence

## Purpose

This directory maps the technical capabilities of the LCNC Security Governance MVP to recognized cybersecurity and governance frameworks.

The mappings are intended to demonstrate how the platform can support an organization's security governance, risk-management, assurance, and audit activities.

They do not represent certification, legal advice, or a claim that deployment of this software makes an organization compliant with any framework or standard.

## Frameworks

### ISO/IEC 27001

The project uses ISO/IEC 27001 as an information-security management and risk-governance reference.

The MVP provides technical evidence that can support activities such as:

- application and asset visibility
- information-security risk assessment
- risk treatment
- accountability
- security policy enforcement
- monitoring
- evidence retention
- secure change practices
- continual improvement

Organizational ISO/IEC 27001 conformity also requires management-system activities, organizational scope, policies, leadership, competence, risk acceptance, internal audit, management review, and other processes outside this MVP.

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

The project maps capabilities across the CSF 2.0 Functions:

- GOVERN
- IDENTIFY
- PROTECT
- DETECT
- RESPOND
- RECOVER

The strongest coverage in this MVP is GOVERN, IDENTIFY, PROTECT, and DETECT, with selected RESPOND and RECOVER capabilities demonstrated through remediation and reassessment.

### OWASP Citizen Development Top 10

The platform directly addresses several citizen-development risks through:

- application discovery
- asset inventory
- data classification
- explainable risk assessment
- policy-as-code
- governance workflow
- monitoring
- CI/CD security validation

The primary mapped OWASP Citizen Development risks are:

- CD-SEC-01 Blind Trust
- CD-SEC-04 Sensitive Data Leakage and Handling Failures
- CD-SEC-06 Vulnerable and Untrusted Components
- CD-SEC-07 Security Misconfiguration
- CD-SEC-09 Asset Management Failures
- CD-SEC-10 Security Logging and Monitoring Failures

## Responsibility Boundary

The platform automates evidence collection and technical governance controls.

It does not replace:

- executive risk ownership
- business accountability
- legal or regulatory interpretation
- internal audit
- management review
- formal risk acceptance
- certification or external assurance

Technology supports the governance process; accountability remains with people and the organization.
